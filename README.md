## The entry

```
In Rookhaven today, the wind has been rescheduled to blow from behind you until 9am. Management apologises for the convenience.
```

127 characters (limit 140).

## How it works

```
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` validates the text (1-140 characters, no leading/trailing whitespace, no control characters), then writes it to `entry.txt` inside `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) with no trailing newline, and reads the file back to confirm it is byte-exact.

## Why this line

1. **Invented city, weather is the point.** Rookhaven does not exist, and the whole line is a forecast: wind, timing, direction.
2. **The smile.** The dry bureaucratic turn: wind is "rescheduled", and the apology is for "the convenience", not the inconvenience.
3. **Broadcast register.** "In Rookhaven today..." is the standard opener of a regional forecast, delivered straight-faced.
4. **Concrete detail.** Wind from behind you, until 9am, exactly what a commuter wants to know.
5. **Every word earns its place.** No filler; 127 characters, and the last two words carry the joke.
